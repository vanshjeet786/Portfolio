# 02_PORTFOLIO_OBJECTIVE.md

# Portfolio Objective

---

## Purpose

The purpose of this portfolio is not to showcase projects.

The purpose is to communicate how Vanshjeet Singh thinks.

Projects exist as evidence.

The website itself is the product.

The visitor should gradually discover the creator through carefully designed experiences rather than being presented with a list of accomplishments.

---

# Mission

Create a digital experience that demonstrates product thinking through storytelling, engineering, interaction and visual communication.

The portfolio should become a conversation starter rather than a résumé replacement.

---

# Success Metrics

The portfolio succeeds if visitors leave with these impressions.

## Engineering Manager

"This person understands architecture and product thinking."

---

## Founder

"This person can build complete products and solve ambiguous problems."

---

## Recruiter

"This candidate is memorable."

---

## Designer

"This engineer cares deeply about user experience."

---

## Frontend Engineer

"The interaction quality is intentional and technically impressive."

---

## Product Manager

"This person understands users before writing code."

---

# What This Portfolio Must Communicate

The website should repeatedly reinforce five ideas.

---

## 1.

Problems come first.

Technology comes second.

---

## 2.

Engineering is storytelling.

Users should understand systems without reading documentation.

---

## 3.

Every product exists because it solves a real problem.

Nothing should feel like a demo project.

---

## 4.

Beautiful interaction should make products easier to understand.

Never harder.

---

## 5.

Curiosity drives every project.

Every repository should feel like an exploration rather than an assignment.

---

# Primary Goal

Create trust.

Not admiration.

Trust.

Visitors should believe

"I would trust this person with an important product."

---

# Secondary Goal

Create curiosity.

The visitor should want to click.

Not because of animations.

Because they genuinely want to understand the work.

---

# Emotional Journey

The experience should naturally move through these stages.

Curiosity

↓

Interest

↓

Understanding

↓

Respect

↓

Conversation

The portfolio should never attempt to create excitement through spectacle alone.

---

# Portfolio Positioning

This website is positioned as

An Interactive Engineering Narrative

rather than

A Portfolio

A Case Study Collection

A Developer Showcase

An Awards Submission

---

# Success Criteria

A successful visitor should understand

Who Vanshjeet is.

How he thinks.

Why he built each product.

How he approaches engineering.

Why those approaches matter.

Without reading large paragraphs.

---

# Non Goals

The portfolio should not attempt to

Teach React.

Teach Three.js.

List every technology.

Display every repository.

Explain every implementation detail.

Win design awards.

Instead,

it should inspire meaningful conversations.

---

# North Star

Every future design decision should answer one question.

Does this help someone understand how Vanshjeet thinks?

If the answer is no,

remove it.
