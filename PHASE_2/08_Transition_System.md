# Phase-2-Experience/08_TRANSITION_SYSTEM.md

# Transition System

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

Transitions connect ideas.

Without transitions,

the portfolio becomes a collection of unrelated pages.

With thoughtful transitions,

the portfolio becomes one continuous experience.

---

# Philosophy

Nothing should suddenly appear.

Nothing should suddenly disappear.

Every transition should answer

"What changed?"

and

"Why?"

Transitions should preserve memory.

Not erase it.

---

# Transition Hierarchy

Micro

↓

Component

↓

Section

↓

World

↓

Journey

Each level has different responsibilities.

---

# Micro Transitions

Duration

150–300ms

Purpose

Communicate interaction.

Examples

Hover.

Button.

Cursor.

Text emphasis.

Image focus.

Micro transitions should feel immediate.

Never playful.

---

# Component Transitions

Duration

300–600ms

Purpose

Reveal information.

Cards.

Expandable panels.

Glass overlays.

Architecture diagrams.

Maintain context.

Never replace.

Reveal.

---

# Section Transitions

Duration

800–1500ms

Purpose

Move between ideas.

Typography exits.

Lighting evolves.

Camera advances.

Objects transform.

The visitor always understands

why the next section exists.

---

# World Transitions

Duration

2–4 seconds

Purpose

Change emotional atmosphere.

Every world should inherit one element from the previous world.

Examples

Career Compass

Constellation

↓

Skillometer

Neural network

↓

Exiles

Communication graph

↓

Leaderboard

Data packets

↓

Stance

Structured forms dissolve into anatomical precision

Transitions should tell stories.

Not merely animate.

---

# Hero Object Transformation

Each world contains one hero object.

Whenever possible,

hero objects should evolve rather than disappear.

Transformation communicates continuity.

Replacement communicates disconnection.

Prefer evolution.

---

# Typography Transitions

Never fade paragraphs independently.

Treat text as editorial composition.

Groups move together.

Headlines receive priority.

Supporting text follows.

Hierarchy remains intact.

---

# Lighting Transitions

Lighting changes before colour.

Visitors notice atmosphere before palette.

As worlds change,

lighting should gradually adapt.

Avoid sudden dramatic changes.

---

# Material Transitions

Glass becomes metal.

Metal becomes graphite.

Graphite becomes wood.

Wood becomes paper.

Materials should evolve naturally.

Never randomly.

---

# Camera Transitions

Acceleration

↓

Travel

↓

Arrival

↓

Pause

Never begin immediately.

Never stop abruptly.

The pause after arrival is essential.

It gives the visitor time to orient themselves.

---

# Sound Transitions (Optional)

If ambient sound exists,

it should crossfade.

Never restart.

Never cut.

Silence is preferable to poor audio.

---

# Performance Rules

Never transition more objects than necessary.

Maintain frame rate.

Prefer quality over quantity.

Visitors remember smoothness.

Not complexity.

---

# Forbidden Transitions

Flash cuts.

Random zooms.

Excessive blur.

Constant rotation.

Page wipes.

Spinning cameras.

Artificial glitches.

Explosions without narrative purpose.

Transitions that exist only to impress.

---

# Success

A visitor should never consciously think,

"That was a nice transition."

They should simply feel that moving through the portfolio was effortless.

The best transition is the one that quietly preserves understanding.
