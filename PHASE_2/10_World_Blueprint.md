# Purpose

This document establishes the blueprint shared by every project world.

Every world is unique.

Every world also belongs to the same portfolio.

Consistency should come from structure,

not appearance.

# Interaction Budget

Every world receives

One hero interaction.

One expansion interaction.

One memorable transition.

No more.

Restraint creates identity.

---

# Lighting Strategy

Every world owns

one lighting signature.

Never reuse lighting.

Atmosphere communicates identity.

---

# Transition Rule

Every world leaves behind one visual memory.

That memory becomes the foundation of the next world.

Nothing should disappear completely.

Everything evolves.

---

# Success

Visitors should feel

"I travelled through five different places,

yet somehow they all belonged together."
