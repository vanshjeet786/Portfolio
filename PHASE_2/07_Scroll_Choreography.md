# Phase-2-Experience/07_SCROLL_CHOREOGRAPHY.md

# Scroll Choreography

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

Scrolling is the primary interaction model of the portfolio.

It should never feel like moving through a webpage.

It should feel like moving through physical space.

The scroll wheel becomes the camera dolly.

The page becomes a stage.

---

# Philosophy

Every scroll input should produce a meaningful response.

Never move content for the sake of movement.

Every pixel travelled should communicate progression.

Scrolling should reveal.

Not transport.

---

# Scroll Characteristics

Movement should feel

Heavy

Controlled

Elastic

Natural

Never robotic.

Never abrupt.

Momentum should resemble pushing a physical object.

Not dragging pixels.

---

# Three Scroll States

## State 01

Observation

The user is reading.

Camera movement is almost imperceptible.

Objects breathe gently.

Nothing competes for attention.

---

## State 02

Transition

The user moves between ideas.

Camera slowly advances.

Lighting evolves.

The environment changes gradually.

Typography exits naturally.

New information enters before old information disappears.

Visitors never lose orientation.

---

## State 03

Discovery

The user reaches an important moment.

Scrolling temporarily slows.

The portfolio encourages observation.

Important ideas deserve time.

Never rush significant transitions.

---

# Scroll Speed

Not every section should consume the same amount of scrolling.

Career Compass

Medium

Skillometer

Long

Exiles

Medium

Leaderboard

Short

Stance

Medium

Creator

Short

The scrolling distance communicates importance.

---

# Camera Movement

Avoid vertical scrolling whenever possible.

Prefer

Forward movement.

Lateral movement.

Subtle rotation.

Parallax with purpose.

Depth progression.

The visitor should feel they are travelling through environments.

Not sliding through containers.

---

# Layered Motion

Foreground

Moves slowly.

Midground

Responds naturally.

Background

Barely moves.

This creates believable depth.

Avoid exaggerated parallax.

---

# Scroll Anchors

Every world should contain one anchor moment.

Anchor moments temporarily reduce movement.

The visitor naturally pauses.

Examples

Cube transformation.

Compass alignment.

Network growth.

Packet validation.

Anatomical reveal.

Portrait.

These become memorable scenes.

---

# Scroll Continuity

Objects leaving one world should influence the next.

Examples

Constellation lines become neural pathways.

Neural pathways become communication signals.

Signals become score packets.

Industrial geometry dissolves into gallery architecture.

Every transition should preserve narrative continuity.

---

# Scroll Behaviour On Mobile

Reduce camera movement.

Increase readability.

Maintain progression.

Avoid complex gestures.

Never sacrifice understanding for spectacle.

---

# Accessibility

Respect reduced-motion settings.

Replace long camera movement with subtle opacity, scale and layout changes.

The story must remain identical.

Only the presentation changes.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Never pin every section.

Never use endless horizontal scrolling.

Never create fake loading moments.

Never make scrolling feel heavy through poor optimisation.

Never disconnect scroll speed from narrative importance.

---

# Success

Visitors should feel that scrolling controls the pace of a story,

not the position of a webpage.
