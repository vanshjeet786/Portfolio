# Purpose

This document defines the complete visitor experience.

It is intentionally independent of implementation.

Think of this as directing a film rather than building a website.

The visitor should never think

"I reached another section."

Instead they should feel

"I've entered another chapter."

---

# Arrival

Objective

Capture attention without demanding it.

Experience

The page feels almost empty.

The cube exists.

The interface waits.

Nothing is trying to sell anything.

Visitors become curious.

---

# Identity

Objective

Answer

Who built this?

Reveal only

Name

Role

Focus statement

Nothing more.

Do not introduce projects.

Do not introduce technology.

---

# Purpose

Objective

Reveal motivation.

"I love solving problems."

Everything after this sentence becomes supporting evidence.

---

# Transition Philosophy

Transitions should feel physical.

Not digital.

Visitors should never feel transported.

Instead,

spaces should naturally evolve into one another.

Think architecture.

Not webpages.

---

# World 01

Career Compass

Emotion

Hope

Question

"How do we help people find direction?"

The visitor should leave understanding that engineering can improve important life decisions.

---

# Transition

Constellation lines slowly reorganise.

The world changes.

Nothing cuts.

Nothing fades abruptly.

---

# World 02

Skillometer

Emotion

Understanding

Question

"How can organisations understand people better?"

This becomes the largest world.

Visitors spend the most time here.

The world should feel alive.

Growing.

Adaptive.

Organic.

---

# Transition

The organic network slowly reorganises into communication pathways.

Connections become messages.

---

# World 03

Exiles

Emotion

Presence

Question

"What makes communication feel effortless?"

The visitor should understand realtime systems visually.

Messages become movement.

Connections become trust.

---

# Transition

Signals accelerate.

Communication transforms into competition.

---

# World 04

Leaderboard

Emotion

Precision

Question

"How do we ensure fairness?"

The atmosphere becomes faster.

More mechanical.

More structured.

Movement becomes directional.

The visitor should understand validation before ranking.

---

# Transition

The system becomes calmer.

Industrial geometry dissolves.

Lighting softens.

---

# World 05

Stance

Emotion

Craftsmanship

Question

"How can interaction improve understanding?"

This becomes the quietest world.

Slow.

Elegant.

Minimal.

Everything feels intentional.

---

# Reflection

Visitors have now seen

AI.

Enterprise.

Communication.

Backend.

Interactive Frontend.

Patterns emerge.

Visitors stop evaluating projects.

They begin evaluating the creator.

---

# Creator

Now introduce the person.

Portrait.

Resume.

Links.

No long biography.

The projects have already introduced the creator.

---

# Conversation

The experience never ends.

It simply becomes quieter.

Visitors leave feeling invited.

Not persuaded.

---

# Experience Rules

Every world

One emotion.

One question.

One memorable interaction.

One dominant visual idea.

Never more.

---

# Success

Visitors should remember

how they felt,

not where they clicked.
