Author: Vanshjeet Singh & ChatGPT

# Introduction

This repository does **not** exist to build a portfolio.

It exists to build the digital identity of a Product Engineer.

The resulting website should communicate how the creator thinks, solves problems, designs systems, builds products and approaches engineering.

Every design decision, animation, interaction and line of code should reinforce that objective.

The portfolio should be treated like a product launch rather than a personal website.

---

# Vision

The website should leave visitors with one lasting impression:

> "This person understands products."

Not

> "This person knows React."

Not

> "This person made a cool website."

Not

> "This person likes animations."

Animations exist to communicate.

Design exists to communicate.

Engineering exists to communicate.

Everything serves the story.

---

# Core Philosophy

The portfolio should answer one question.

> Why should someone trust Vanshjeet Singh to build products?

The answer should never rely on claims.

The answer should emerge naturally through the experience.

The visitor should gradually discover

- the creator
- the projects
- the engineering
- the product thinking
- the personality

instead of reading them.

---

# Design Principles

Every decision throughout this specification should satisfy these principles.

## 1. Story over spectacle

Animations should explain.

Never decorate.

---

## 2. Curiosity over information

Do not show everything immediately.

Reward exploration.

---

## 3. Simplicity over complexity

Every screen should communicate one primary idea.

---

## 4. Systems over features

Projects should showcase thinking.

Not feature lists.

---

## 5. Authenticity over marketing

Never exaggerate.

Never fabricate.

Never use unnecessary buzzwords.

---

## 6. Performance over novelty

Maintain smooth interactions.

Every effect must justify its cost.

---

## 7. Timelessness over trends

Avoid visual clichés.

Avoid AI stereotypes.

Avoid anything likely to feel outdated within a few years.

---
