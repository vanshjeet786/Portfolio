# CREATIVE CONTINUITY

Before implementing this world:

Spend at least 10 minutes navigating the existing experience.

Do not treat this as a new page.

Treat it as the next room in the same building.

Your objective is not to build another project.

Your objective is to extend the same exhibition.

Visitors should never feel that they left one webpage and entered another.

Every transition should preserve emotional continuity.

The world should inherit:

- composition
- pacing
- material language
- lighting philosophy
- interaction language
- typography rhythm

from the previous world while expressing its own identity.

If your implementation feels like a separate website, start again.
