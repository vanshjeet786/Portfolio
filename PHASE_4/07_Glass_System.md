# Phase-4-Interaction-Language/07_GLASS_SYSTEM.md

# Glass System

Version 1.0

---

# Philosophy

Glass communicates depth.

Not trend.

Use restraint.

Glass should feel premium because it is rare.

---

# Principles

Glass never becomes a page.

Glass never becomes a background.

Glass highlights interaction.

---

# Allowed Usage

Navigation.

Floating cards.

Project expansion.

Image overlays.

Resume preview.

Context panels.

---

# Forbidden Usage

Entire layouts.

Full-page glass.

Glass buttons everywhere.

Heavy blur.

Strong transparency.

---

# Material

Soft frost.

Gentle blur.

Natural reflection.

Subtle edge lighting.

Minimal tint.

---

# Behaviour

Glass reacts to light.

Not colour.

Reflections evolve as the camera moves.

Never shimmer excessively.

---

# Layering

Environment

↓

Glass

↓

Typography

↓

Cursor

Glass separates.

It never dominates.

---

# Performance

Reuse shaders.

Limit blur radius.

Avoid multiple overlapping layers.

Optimise aggressively.

---

# Accessibility

Maintain readable contrast.

Typography always wins over transparency.

Reduced transparency available if required.

---

# Success

Glass should feel like architecture.

Not a design trend.
