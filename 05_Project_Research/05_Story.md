# 07_STORY.md

# The Story

Version 1.0

---

# Introduction

This portfolio should not be structured like a résumé.

It should be structured like a story.

Stories are memorable.

Lists are forgotten.

The visitor should feel they have met the creator.

Not viewed a collection of projects.

---

# Theme

"I love solving problems."

This sentence becomes the emotional centre of the experience.

Everything after this should prove it.

Never repeat the sentence.

Demonstrate it.

---

# Act I

## Curiosity

Silence.

Minimal interface.

The cube.

Slow movement.

Whitespace.

No explanations.

The visitor asks

"What is this?"

That question should naturally become

"Who built this?"

---

# Act II

## Identity

The creator appears.

Name.

Role.

One sentence.

Nothing more.

Do not overwhelm.

Allow curiosity to continue.

---

# Act III

## Purpose

"I love solving problems."

The visitor now understands motivation.

Not skills.

Motivation.

Projects now become evidence.

---

# Act IV

## Exploration

Every project becomes another world.

Not another section.

Every transition communicates

New problem.

New environment.

New way of thinking.

Yet everything belongs to one larger story.

---

# Act V

## Understanding

As visitors continue,

they stop learning about projects.

They begin learning about the creator.

Patterns emerge.

Product thinking.

Systems thinking.

Curiosity.

Craftsmanship.

Consistency.

Visitors realise these qualities appear repeatedly.

---

# Act VI

## Trust

By now

Technology becomes secondary.

The visitor begins trusting judgement.

Not simply implementation.

This is the most important transition.

The portfolio no longer proves competence.

It earns confidence.

---

# Act VII

## Invitation

The ending should feel quiet.

No dramatic conclusion.

No sales pitch.

Simply

A portrait.

Links.

Resume.

One sentence.

> Let's build something meaningful.

The conversation begins after the portfolio ends.

---

# Narrative Structure

Every project follows

Problem

↓

Observation

↓

Approach

↓

Engineering

↓

Outcome

↓

Reflection

This structure should never change.

Consistency creates familiarity.

---

# Emotional Curve

Curiosity

↓

Wonder

↓

Interest

↓

Understanding

↓

Respect

↓

Trust

↓

Conversation

Notice

Excitement is not part of the curve.

Trust matters more.

---

# What The Visitor Should Remember

Not

The cube.

Not

The animations.

Not

The libraries.

They should remember

"This person understands products."

Everything else is supporting evidence.

---

# Final Sentence

The portfolio is successful if someone closes the tab and immediately thinks of a problem they would like Vanshjeet Singh to solve.
