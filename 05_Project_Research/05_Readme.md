# Project Research

This directory contains the complete research for every featured project.

These documents are **not** case studies.

They are the foundation for every future design, animation, interaction and narrative throughout the portfolio.

Every project should be treated as its own world.

Every world should answer one question:

> "What problem does this project solve, and why should anyone care?"

Technology is intentionally placed lower in priority.

People remember stories.

People remember problems.

People remember elegant solutions.

They rarely remember frameworks.

---

# Research Philosophy

Every project should communicate four things.

## Layer 1

Recognition

"What is this?"

---

## Layer 2

Understanding

"Why was it built?"

---

## Layer 3

Engineering

"How was it built?"

---

## Layer 4

Thinking

"Why was it built this way instead of another way?"

---

# Identity Cards

Each project receives an Identity Card.

Identity Cards are creative constraints.

They influence

- world design
- lighting
- motion
- pacing
- interaction
- transitions
- colour
- typography

They are not visible to visitors.

They exist to maintain consistency.

---

# Animation Philosophy

Animations should explain engineering.

Never decorate engineering.

Examples

Good

Leaderboard requests travelling through validation before entering rankings.

Bad

Random floating cubes around a leaderboard.

---

# Story Philosophy

Every project follows the same structure.

Problem

↓

Observation

↓

Approach

↓

Engineering

Outcome

Visitors should naturally understand why a project exists before discovering how it works.
