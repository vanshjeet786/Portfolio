# Executive Summary

Skillometer is the largest and most ambitious project in this portfolio.

It should become the emotional centre of the website.

Skillometer is not simply an assessment platform.

It is a modular evaluation ecosystem.

It combines assessment, AI, role mapping, organizations, reporting, analytics and enterprise workflows into a unified platform.

The portfolio should communicate scale without overwhelming the visitor.

# Problem

Traditional assessments reduce people to scores.

Organizations struggle to evaluate capability fairly.

Most systems ignore context.

Most systems are static.

Skillometer attempts to create a richer understanding of human capability.

# Why This Exists

To build an intelligent evaluation platform that balances structured assessments with adaptive reasoning.

The platform is designed to evolve.
