# Executive Summary

Career Compass is an AI-assisted career guidance platform that helps users understand themselves before recommending careers.

The project is not a career recommendation website.

It is a decision support system.

The engineering challenge was never recommending careers.

It was reducing uncertainty in career decisions.

The portfolio should communicate that distinction immediately.

# Problem

Career guidance today is often generic.

Most platforms rely on static questionnaires, personality labels or keyword matching.

They rarely explain *why* a recommendation was made.

Users leave with answers but little confidence.

---

# Why This Exists

Career Compass exists to increase confidence in career decisions.

Instead of asking

"What career should I choose?"

it asks

"Who are you becoming?"

Recommendations become the result of understanding rather than prediction.
