# Navigation Rules

Navigation should never interrupt the experience.

It should quietly assist it.

Users should always know

where they are,

where they came from,

and

what remains.

Navigation should never dominate.

---

# Camera Rules

Camera movement should create continuity.

Visitors should never feel teleported.

Every transition should preserve orientation.

Movement should communicate progression.

---

# World Rules

Projects are worlds.

Not pages.

Each world should possess

its own atmosphere,

its own rhythm,

its own interaction,

its own lighting,

while remaining visually related.

---

# Progressive Disclosure

Every world must reveal information gradually.

Level 1

Recognition

↓

Level 2

Understanding

↓

Level 3

Engineering

↓

Level 4

Technical depth

Do not reverse this order.
