# Problem

Healthcare websites are often overloaded with text, stock imagery and poor interaction.

Patients struggle to understand treatments and medical concepts.

Medical products deserve better storytelling.

---

# Why This Exists

The objective was to create a premium digital experience that communicates healthcare visually.

Rather than overwhelming users with information, the website guides them through carefully designed interactions.

Three-dimensional models become educational tools rather than decorative elements.

# Product Thinking

The objective was never to build a visually impressive website.

The objective was to increase clarity and engagement while supporting discoverability through strong SEO.

Visual quality becomes a business tool rather than a design exercise.

# Motion Opportunities

The visitor should feel like they are walking through an exhibition.

Camera movement should resemble a museum guide.

Objects should reveal themselves gradually.

Nothing should suddenly appear.

Lighting changes should communicate focus.

---

# 3D Opportunities

Use the actual anatomical assets from the repository.

Allow subtle interaction.

Never turn the experience into a 3D playground.

The model exists to explain.

Not entertain.
