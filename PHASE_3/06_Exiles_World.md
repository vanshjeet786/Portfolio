# Phase-3-Production-Design/06_EXILES_WORLD.md

# Exiles World

Version 1.0

---

# World Identity

Name

The Network

Theme

Connection

Purpose

Communication

Presence

Unlike previous worlds,

this one feels active.

Not noisy.

Alive.

---

# Emotional Goal

Visitors should understand

that good communication systems disappear.

Only conversations remain.

---

# World Narrative

Question

How should digital communication feel?

↓

Observation

Users should never think about infrastructure.

↓

Exploration

Communication is movement.

↓

Engineering

Realtime architecture.

↓

Outcome

Invisible complexity.

---

# Hero Object

Signal Network.

Not chat bubbles.

Not message cards.

Signals.

Invisible pathways.

Relationship lines.

Presence pulses.

Visitors see communication

before

they see messages.

---

# Environment

Dark architectural volume.

Signals travel across space.

Nodes represent people.

Messages become light.

No futuristic holograms.

Everything feels grounded.

---

# Lighting

Soft amber.

Deep graphite.

Directional highlights.

Signals illuminate space temporarily.

Nothing permanently glows.

---

# Materials

Steel.

Walnut.

Graphite.

Amber Glass.

Fabric.

Natural surfaces.

---

# Colour

Deep Forest

Warm Black

Amber

Graphite

Muted Steel

---

# Camera

The camera quietly follows communication.

Not users.

Movement should resemble documentary filmmaking.

Never dramatic.

---

# Motion

Signals travel.

Presence breathes.

Connections strengthen.

Latency becomes visible.

Messages leave subtle trails.

Nothing feels instantaneous.

Everything has travel time.

This makes the infrastructure understandable.

---

# User Interaction

Hover

Reveal relationships.

Click

Expand conversations.

Scroll

Move through communication layers.

Expand

Realtime architecture unfolds beneath the experience.
