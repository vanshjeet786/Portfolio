# Phase-3-Production-Design/08_STANCE_WORLD.md

# Stance World

Version 1.0

---

# World Identity

Name

The Gallery

Theme

Craftsmanship

Purpose

Understanding

Unlike every previous world,

this one is almost silent.

Visitors should instinctively slow down.

---

# Emotional Goal

Visitors should feel

"This is beautifully considered."

Not

"This is technically impressive."

Beauty exists to improve understanding.

---

# World Narrative

Question

Can interaction improve healthcare communication?

↓

Observation

Medical information overwhelms.

↓

Exploration

Interaction guides attention.

↓

Engineering

Three-dimensional storytelling.

↓

Outcome

Understanding.

---

# Hero Object

The Anatomical Sculpture.

Use the actual model.

Never replace it.

Never stylise it.

It becomes the centrepiece of the room.

Lighting should celebrate it.

Not dramatise it.

---

# Environment

A contemporary museum.

Travertine.

Limestone.

Natural oak.

Fabric.

Glass.

Quiet architecture.

Visitors should almost hear silence.

---

# Lighting

Morning light.

Soft skylight.

Indirect shadows.

Natural reflections.

Everything feels believable.

---

# Materials

Travertine.

Linen.

Natural Glass.

Oak.

Aluminium.

Paper.

No glossy plastic.

---

# Colour

Stone White.

Warm Grey.

Sage.

Natural Oak.

Soft Blue.

---

# Camera

Museum pacing.

Very slow.

Purposeful.

Almost invisible.

The camera should respect the object.

Never orbit dramatically.

---

# Motion

Lighting evolves.

Small labels appear.

Camera shifts gently.

Objects reveal themselves.

The world should feel alive without appearing animated.

---

# User Interaction

Hover

Highlight anatomy.

Click

Reveal explanation.

Scroll

Advance through the story.

Expand

Open implementation details.

Three.js.

GSAP.

SEO.

Architecture.

Museum walls slowly dissolve.

Only light remains.

Typography becomes dominant.

Visitors leave products.

They approach the creator.
