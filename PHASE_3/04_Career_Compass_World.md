# Phase-3-Production-Design/04_CAREER_COMPASS_WORLD.md

# Career Compass World

Version 1.0

---

# World Identity

Name

The Observatory

Theme

Direction

Purpose

Hope

Discovery

This is the first project world.

It teaches visitors how to experience every world that follows.

It should immediately establish that projects are not presented as case studies.

They are explored.

---

# Emotional Goal

Visitors should feel

"I know where I am going."

Not because careers have been recommended.

Because they understand how the product thinks.

---

# World Narrative

Question

How do we help someone make one of the most important decisions of their life?

↓

Observation

People are overwhelmed.

↓

Exploration

Understanding comes before recommendation.

↓

Engineering

Assessments become systems.

↓

Outcome

Recommendations become believable.

---

# Hero Object

A Compass.

Not a decorative compass.

A living instrument.

Heavy.

Elegant.

Mechanically believable.

It slowly searches for direction.

The visitor never controls it.

Instead,

the compass reacts to the visitor's journey.

---

# Environment

An observatory.

Large circular room.

Stone floor.

Brass instruments.

Paper charts.

Constellations.

Soft atmospheric haze.

Large amount of empty space.

Everything feels handcrafted.

---

# Lighting

Warm evening light.

Moonlight entering through the ceiling.

Soft brass reflections.

Very subtle moving shadows.

Nothing glows.

---

# Materials

Brass.

Stone.

Glass.

Paper.

Aged leather.

Wood.

No polished chrome.

No neon.

---

# Colour

Midnight Blue

Brass

Cream

Warm White

Muted Gold

The colours should communicate trust.

Not technology.

---

# Camera

The camera moves in circles.

Very slowly.

Almost like observing the night sky.

No sudden zooms.

No aggressive movement.

---

# Motion

Nothing rotates continuously.

The compass occasionally aligns.

Constellations connect.

Small particles drift.

Charts gently move.

Everything feels patient.

---

# User Interaction

Hover

The compass reacts.

Scroll

The observatory slowly evolves.

Expand

The assessment architecture unfolds beneath the compass.

No popups.

No modals.

Everything expands naturally.

---

# Engineering Reveal

Visitors eventually discover

Assessment Pipeline

↓

Evaluation

↓

AI Assistance

↓

Recommendation Engine

↓

Career Output

The engineering should emerge from beneath the observatory.

Almost like discovering hidden machinery.

---

# Screens Required

Landing page.

Assessment flow.

Recommendation screen.

Dashboard.

Results.

Only real screenshots.

---

# World Exit

Constellation lines slowly reorganise.

The stars begin forming nodes.

The visitor unknowingly enters Skillometer.
