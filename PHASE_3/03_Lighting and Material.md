# Lighting & Material System

Version 1.0

---

# Philosophy

People recognise places through lighting before colour.

Therefore,

lighting becomes one of the strongest storytelling tools in the portfolio.

---

# Lighting Hierarchy

Environment

↓

Hero Object

↓

Typography

↓

Interface

The interface should never be the brightest object.

---

# Natural Lighting

Prefer

Soft directional light.

Bounce light.

Ambient shadow.

Natural reflections.

Avoid

Artificial bloom.

Heavy emission.

Extreme contrast.

---

# Hero Lighting

Every world contains

one light source

that subtly directs attention.

Visitors should instinctively know

where to look.

Without arrows.

Without highlights.

# Material Behaviour

Materials should react naturally to movement.

Reflections remain subtle.

No mirror-like chrome.

No exaggerated refraction.

Everything should feel believable.

# Shadow Philosophy

Shadows communicate depth.

Not darkness.

Soft.

Natural.

Readable.

Avoid dramatic noir lighting.

---

# Atmosphere

Every world should possess

air.

Distance.

Silence.

Depth.

The visitor should feel immersed,

not surrounded by visual effects.

---

# Final Principle

Lighting should guide attention.

Materials should communicate emotion.

Together,

they should explain the personality of each project before the visitor reads a single word.
