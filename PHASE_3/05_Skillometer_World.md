# Phase-3-Production-Design/05_SKILLOMETER_WORLD.md

# Skillometer World

Version 1.0

---

# World Identity

Name

The Living System

Theme

Understanding People

Purpose

Intelligence

Growth

This is the largest world.

It should become the emotional centrepiece of the portfolio.

---

# Emotional Goal

Visitors should feel

"This system is alive."

Not visually.

Intellectually.

Every interaction should communicate adaptation.

---

# World Narrative

Question

How do organisations truly understand people?

↓

Observation

Traditional assessments simplify people.

↓

Exploration

Capability is multidimensional.

↓

Engineering

Adaptive systems.

↓

Outcome

Meaningful evaluation.

---

# Hero Object

Living Intelligence.

Not a brain.

Not AI.

Instead,

an organic network.

Nodes appear alive.

Connections strengthen.

Relationships evolve.

Nothing feels mechanical.

---

# Environment

Dark architectural space.

Organic structures emerge from the floor.

Copper pathways.

Graphite walls.

Translucent membranes.

Floating evaluation layers.

Everything feels designed.

Not biological.

---

# Lighting

Soft copper highlights.

Dark ambient atmosphere.

Light travels through the network.

Visitors follow light.

Not arrows.

---

# Materials

Graphite.

Copper.

Clay.

Organic glass.

Stone.

Minimal reflections.

---

# Colour

Graphite

Copper

Warm White

Stone Grey

Muted Clay

No bright colours.

No gradients used for decoration.

---

# Camera

Slow exploration.

Occasionally moves through the network.

Never flies aggressively.

Visitors should feel

inside

the system.

---

# Motion

Connections strengthen.

Nodes breathe.

Networks reorganise.

Nothing loops obviously.

Everything responds naturally.

---

# Gooey Interaction

This becomes the gooey world.

Organic surfaces merge.

Separate.

Stretch.

Reconnect.

Cursor movement influences viscosity.

Scroll changes network density.

The effect should remain subtle.

Never playful.

---

# User Interaction

Hover

Nodes reveal relationships.

Click

Expand architecture.

Scroll

System evolves.

Expand

Enterprise workflows appear.

Nothing interrupts immersion.
